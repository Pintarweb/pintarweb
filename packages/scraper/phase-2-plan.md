# 💎 PintarWeb Intelligence Engine: Phase 2 Roadmap

This roadmap outlines the evolution of the Lead Intelligence Engine into a high-conversion sales factory for the Malaysian SME market.

---

## 🟢 Stage 1: Conversion & Personalization
**Objective**: Turn lead data into high-converting messages.
*   **Feature**: **Dynamic WhatsApp Pitch Templates**.
    *   Implement selections for: "Security Expert" (SSL Focus), "Speed King" (Perf Focus), and "Digital Architect" (No-Website Special).
    *   *Result*: Higher response rates through context-aware outreach.

## 🟡 Stage 2: Visual Intelligence
**Objective**: Enhance dashboard aesthetics and prepare sales assets.
*   **Feature**: **Logo & Hero Asset Extraction**.
    *   Update Playwright scrapers to identify and store business logos and main product images.
    *   Display these in the Dashboard lead cards.
    *   *Result*: A premium "Gallery Mode" dashboard and pre-collected assets for demo builds.

## 🟠 Stage 3: Operational Discipline
**Objective**: Manage high-volume lead flows without data loss.
*   **Feature**: **CRM Integration & Follow-up Scheduling**.
    *   "Export to CRM" button for leads with Score > 10.
    *   Automated daily summary email of "Hot Leads" generated in the last 24h.
    *   *Result*: A disciplined, traceable sales funnel.

## 🔴 Stage 4: The 360° Social Audit
**Objective**: Identify the "Ghost SMEs" (High reputation, Low digital tech).
*   **Feature**: **Social Proof Cross-Verification**.
    *   Automated checks for Facebook, Instagram, and LinkedIn presence.
    *   +5 Point "Golden Target" bonus for businesses with high reviews but zero/broken web presence.
    *   *Result*: Perfect identification of businesses most likely to say "Yes."

## 🔥 Stage 5: Zero-Touch Demo Automation
**Objective**: The "End Game" of lead conversion.
*   **Feature**: **One-Click AI Demo Generator**.
    *   A dashboard action that triggers a Stitch/Next.js blueprint build using the lead's assets and AI pain points.
    *   Generate a unique "Demo Link" to include in the WhatsApp pitch.
    *   *Result*: Delivering high-value prototypes in under 60 seconds.
