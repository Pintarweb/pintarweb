# Headline Formulas — Contractor Niche

## Purpose
10 tested headline structures specifically for Malaysian contractor/aircond/trades niches.
These formulas work because they prioritize trust, locality, and clarity over creativity.

## Core Principles
- **Service-first, not brand-first** — what you do matters more than who you are
- **Location-specific** — area name builds trust and relevance
- **Benefit or speed** — what the customer gets, fast
- **No corporate jargon** — practical language only

---

## Formula 1: Service + Location (Default)
**Pattern:** [Service] [Location]

**BM Examples:**
- Pakar Aircond Cheras
- Kontraktor Renovation Shah Alam
- Servis Aircond & Renovation Petaling Jaya

**EN Examples:**
- Aircond Specialist Cheras
- Renovation Contractor Shah Alam
- Aircond Service & Renovation Petaling Jaya

**When to use:** Default choice. Clear, direct, SEO-friendly. Works for 80% of cases.

**Why it works:** Immediately tells visitor what you do and where. No guessing.

---

## Formula 2: Service + Location + Experience
**Pattern:** [Service] [Location] — Sejak [Year]

**BM Examples:**
- Pakar Aircond Cheras — Sejak 2015
- Kontraktor Renovation Shah Alam — Berpengalaman 10+ Tahun
- Servis Plumbing Ampang — Dipercayai Sejak 2012

**EN Examples:**
- Aircond Specialist Cheras — Since 2015
- Renovation Contractor Shah Alam — 10+ Years Experience
- Plumbing Service Ampang — Trusted Since 2012

**When to use:** When established year is a competitive advantage (5+ years).

**Why it works:** Adds instant credibility. "Since 2015" signals longevity and trust.

---

## Formula 3: Your Trusted + Service + Location
**Pattern:** Your Trusted [Service] [Location]

**BM Examples:**
- Pakar Aircond Yang Dipercayai di Cheras
- Kontraktor Renovation Terpercaya Shah Alam

**EN Examples:**
- Your Trusted Aircond Expert in Cheras
- Trusted Renovation Contractor Shah Alam

**When to use:** When testimonials/reviews are strong (rating > 4.0, reviews > 20).

**Why it works:** "Trusted" signals social proof without being boastful.

**Avoid:** Overuse. "Trusted" loses meaning if everyone claims it.

---

## Formula 4: Fast/Same-Day + Service + Location
**Pattern:** [Speed Claim] [Service] [Location]

**BM Examples:**
- Servis Aircond Pantas Cheras — Kami Datang Hari Ini
- Pembaikan Aircond Segera Shah Alam
- Plumbing Kecemasan 24 Jam Petaling Jaya

**EN Examples:**
- Fast Aircond Service Cheras — We Come Today
- Emergency Aircond Repair Shah Alam
- 24-Hour Emergency Plumbing Petaling Jaya

**When to use:** For emergency/urgent services where speed is the differentiator.

**Why it works:** Addresses immediate pain point. "Today" removes friction.

## Translation Pairing Rules

When using these formulas with data-bm and data-en:
- **Do NOT translate word-for-word**
- Each language should sound natural in that language
- BM can be more conversational; EN more professional

### Good Pairing Example:
```html
<h1 class="text-3xl md:text-4xl font-bold">
  <span data-bm="Pakar Aircond & Renovation Cheras"
        data-en="Your Trusted Aircond & Renovation Expert in Cheras">
    Pakar Aircond & Renovation Cheras
  </span>
</h1>
```
Note: BM is direct ("Pakar"), EN adds "Your Trusted" for warmth. Both sound natural.

### Bad Pairing Example:
```html
<!-- Don't do this -->
<span data-bm="Pakar Aircond Cheras"
      data-en="Expert Aircond Cheras">
```
"Expert Aircond Cheras" sounds awkward in English. Better: "Aircond Expert in Cheras"

## What NOT to Write

### Avoid These Patterns:
- ❌ "Transforming Spaces with Innovation" (too corporate)
- ❌ "Your Partner in Excellence" (meaningless)
- ❌ "Premium Solutions for Modern Living" (SaaS language)
- ❌ "Empowering Your Home Improvement Journey" (forbidden words)
- ❌ "Where Quality Meets Affordability" (cliché)

### Why Headlines Fail:
1. **Too abstract** — "Excellence in Service" tells visitor nothing
2. **Too corporate** — "Solutions" and "Partner" are buzzwords
3. **No location** — "Best Aircond Service" (best where?)
4. **No service clarity** — "Home Improvement Experts" (what exactly?)
5. **Fake urgency** — "Limited Time Only!" (when it's not)

## Testing Your Headline

Before finalizing, ask:
1. **Can I tell what service this is in 2 seconds?** (clarity test)
2. **Do I know where this business operates?** (location test)
3. **Would a real Malaysian SME owner say this?** (authenticity test)
4. **Does it differentiate from competitors?** (relevance test)

If any answer is "no" → pick a different formula or adjust.

---

## Implementation

When generating a hero section, select the formula that best matches:
- The niche (emergency vs scheduled service)
- Available data (established year? service areas? rating?)
- Competition (what are others NOT saying?)

Then apply data-bm and data-en with natural translations for each language.
