# No Website Playbook

## The mindset
No website is not a gap in the audit — it IS the audit finding.
Do not treat it as a problem to apologise for. It is the story.

"You're invisible to anyone who isn't already looking for you by name."

## How scores change

Visibility Score — still calculate, just recalibrate:
  Website presence:     0/40  (absent, not failed)
  Google listing:       check
  Facebook/Instagram:   check
  Directories:          check
Result: typically 20–40/100. Let the number tell the story.

First Impression Score — DO NOT score a site that doesn't exist.
Replace entirely with "What customers see right now" section (see below).

Competitor section — becomes the centrepiece, not a supporting table.
Search volume — becomes the opening hook, not a supporting data point.

## "What customers see right now" section (replaces First Impression Score)

✓  Google Maps listing (if they have one)
✓  Phone number
✓  Star rating and reviews
✗  No website to click through to
✗  No service details beyond Google categories
✗  No photos of their work (unless on Google listing)
✗  No way to learn more before committing to call

## Competitor section — make it a direct customer journey comparison

When someone searches "[service] [area]" right now:

Competitor A  ← has website, 4.5★, 67 reviews
  → Customer sees their site, projects, WhatsApp button

Competitor B  ← has website, 4.2★, 43 reviews
  → Customer reads testimonials, clicks call

[Your Lead]   ← no website, 4.3★, 31 reviews
  → Customer hits a dead end. Moves on.

## Search volume as opening hook

"290 people searched 'aircond service Cheras' last month.
None of them found you — not because your work isn't good,
but because there was nowhere for them to land."

## Demo pitch language — no website version

NOT: "We built something to fix this."
USE: "We built what's been missing."

The first implies they had a problem.
The second implies they were almost there — just needed this one piece.
