# Niche Logic

## Aircond & Contractor (primary niche)

### Customer psychology
- Searching after something broke or before hot season
- Decision speed: fast (urgency-driven)
- Trust factors: how fast they respond, local area coverage,
  photos of real work, WhatsApp availability
- Price sensitivity: medium — will pay for reliability

### Layout priority order
1. Hero — service + area + WhatsApp CTA
2. Trust signals — response time, years experience, coverage area
3. Services — clear list, no jargon
4. Gallery — real project photos perform > stock
5. Testimonials — local names and areas matter
6. FAQ — common questions (warranty? 24hrs? areas covered?)
7. Footer — phone, WhatsApp, area list

### Copy tone
- Direct. Fast. Reassuring.
- "Aircond rosak? Kami datang hari ini."
- Not: "We provide comprehensive HVAC solutions."

### What converts
- WhatsApp pre-filled message (removes friction)
- Response time badge ("Reply dalam 1 jam")
- Real before/after project photos
- Local area names in hero subheadline
- Google review count visible

### What doesn't convert
- Long paragraphs about company history
- Generic stock photos
- Complicated navigation
- No visible phone/WhatsApp above fold
