# Social Media Playbook

## The reframe
Do not treat social media as a consolation prize for no website.
Treat it as proof of business health that isn't being monetised properly.

A contractor with 2,000 Instagram followers and consistent project posts
is doing real work and building real trust — just inside a platform
they don't own, can't control, and can't convert from efficiently.

The narrative:
"You've already done the hard work of building credibility.
The problem is it's all locked inside Instagram.
Anyone who doesn't already follow you can't find it."

**The harder truth — "Kena Tipu oleh Facebook":**
Facebook and Instagram are not the contractor's shop. They are borrowing
someone else's shop. When the owner changes the locks (algorithm update),
the contractor loses everything — followers, reach, inquiries — overnight.
A website is the contractor's own shop. Nobody can take it away.

## These are your BEST leads — not your hardest
Why: They already understand that showing their work builds trust.
They have real content. They have an audience.
Your job is not to convince them online presence matters — they know.
Your job is to show them the gap between the audience they HAVE
and the audience they are MISSING.

## Visibility Score — recalibrated for social-active leads

Has website:               +40
Google listing complete:   +20
Instagram active:          +15
TikTok active:             +15
Facebook page:             +10
Directories:               +10 each (max 10)

A business with strong Instagram + TikTok but no website
typically scores 50–55. Honest. They are partially visible
but to the wrong audience and in a channel they don't own.

## New audit section: "Your Social Media is Working Against You"

Frame as structural problem, not personal failure:

"You have [X] followers on Instagram.
Your project posts get real engagement.
That credibility exists — but only for people who already found you.

When someone searches 'aircond service Cheras' on Google,
your Instagram doesn't appear.
When someone clicks your Google listing,
there's no website to land on — just a phone number.

All that work you've put into your content
isn't reaching the people actively searching for your service right now."

## The "link in bio" callout

Check their Instagram bio. Most Malaysian SME tradespeople have:
- No link at all
- A WhatsApp link (good instinct, poor execution)
- A Facebook link (circular — sending social to social)

None of these capture intent-driven traffic.

Callout copy:
"Your Instagram bio sends visitors to WhatsApp directly.
That works for followers who already trust you.
But a stranger who just discovered you isn't ready to message yet.
They want to see your services, your area, your previous work first.
A website gives them that context — then the WhatsApp button."

## The "what you're losing" calculation

"Your Instagram has 2,100 followers.
Each follower found you at some point — they know you exist.

But searches for 'aircond service Cheras' happen 290 times a month.
Those are people actively looking to hire someone right now.
They haven't found you yet — and they won't, until there's
somewhere for them to land.

Social media builds relationships with people who already know you.
A website captures people who are searching for someone exactly like you.
You need both."

## Demo site — social proof layer

Because they have real content, pull it in:
- Screenshot their best Instagram project posts → gallery section
- Pull real testimonials from comments or Google reviews
- Reference their following as a trust signal in the footer
  ("2.1k followers on Instagram")
- Embed TikTok video if quality is strong

This makes the demo feel like THEIR business, not a fictional one.
You are not inventing credibility — you are organising what already exists.

## Outreach message — social-aware version

Reference their Instagram specifically. Proves you looked:

"Hi [Name], nampak [Business] ada Instagram yang active —
project photos nampak bagus. Kami buat quick report pasal
online presence awak + demo website. Boleh tengok:
[URL]"

Mentioning their Instagram:
1. Proves you are not blasting everyone
2. Connects the audit to something they are already proud of
3. Opens with a compliment that is specific and earned, not generic

## "Kena Tipu oleh Facebook" — Content Framework for Outreach

This is the core narrative for LinkedIn/FB content targeting social-active contractors.
Use this in Phase 1 outreach to make them realise their platform dependency is a ticking time bomb.

### The 3-Part Narrative

**Hook (the pain they feel):**
> "Abang pernah perasan tak — post abang kat Facebook dulu dapat 50-60 like, sekarang dapat 5-10? Bukan orang tak minat. Facebook yang sorok. Abang kena tipu."

**Middle (the reframe):**
> "Facebook bukan kedai abang. Facebook macam kedai orang lain yang abang pinjam. Bila dia nak tukar kunci, abang tak boleh buat apa-apa. Website abang = kedai abang sendiri. Takde siapa boleh tutup."

**Close (fear + solution):**
> "Tahun lepas, Facebook tukar algorithm. Post bisnes kecil drop 40-60%. Abang punya page pun sama. Bayangkan kalau semua customer abang ada dalam phone abang sendiri — bukan dalam Facebook yang boleh berubah bila-bila masa."

### 3 Post Templates for LinkedIn / Facebook Groups

**Post 1 — The Algorithm Drop:**
> "Saya check 10 contractor aircond area Cheras yang aktif kat Facebook.
>
> 8 dari 10 — post diorang dulu dapat 50+ like. Sekarang? 5-10 je.
>
> Bukan content diorang teruk. Bukan orang tak minat aircond.
>
> Facebook yang tukar algorithm. Post bisnes kecil yang drop paling teruk.
>
> Customer yang abang ada kat Facebook sekarang — bukan customer abang. Customer abang = orang yang search 'aircond service Cheras' kat Google.
>
> Website = kedai abang sendiri. Facebook = kedai orang yang abang pinjam."

**Post 2 — The Dead End:**
> "Customer search 'aircond service Kajang' kat Google.
>
> Dia nampak page abang. Dia klik.
>
> Takde website. Cuma nombor telefon.
>
> Dia try call. Line busy — abang tengah atas bumbung fix compressor.
>
> Dia scroll ke competitor. Competitor ada website, ada WhatsApp button.
>
> Customer tu bukan hilang sebab abang tak bagus. Dia hilang sebab takde tempat untuk dia landing.
>
> Facebook post abang tak boleh tolong dia. Google yang tentukan siapa muncul."

**Post 3 — The Ownership Reality:**
> "Abang ada 2,000 follower Instagram. Bagus.
>
> Tapi bila Instagram tukar algorithm esok — abang hilang semua tu.
>
> Nama customer, nombor telefon, alamat — semua dalam phone abang ke? Atau dalam Facebook/Instagram?
>
> Customer yang contact melalui website — nama, nombor, alamat semua abang punya. Bukan platform punya.
>
> Website = abang control. Social media = orang lain control."

### 3-Line WhatsApp Pitch (for "data ownership" reframed)

1. "Abang punya Facebook page, orang boleh scroll past. Tapi website abang kat Google — orang yang search 'aircond service Cheras' tu memang NAK service aircond. Beza besar."
2. "Sekarang abang bergantung kat Facebook algorithm. Bila algorithm tukar, inquiry drop. Website = abang control, bukan Zuckerberg."
3. "Customer yang contact melalui website — nama, nombor, alamat semua abang punya. Bukan Facebook punya."

### Content Calendar

1 "Kena Tipu" post per week for the first month. Rotate between the 3 templates. Track which angle gets the most engagement. Use the winning angle as the basis for direct outreach messaging.
