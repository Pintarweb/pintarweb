**What Works (The "Wins")**

* **Localized Authority:** The hero headline "Pakar Elektrik & Rangkaian Dipercayai di Kuala Lumpur" uses high-intent keywords that immediately establish geographical and functional trust for local SMEs.
* **Conversion-First Contact:** Inclusion of a direct WhatsApp button (green) right next to the lead form is a massive win for the Malaysian market, where "PM" (Private Message) is the preferred communication mode.
* **Real Human Element:** The use of a photo featuring a technician in safety gear with a "10+ Tahun Pengalaman" badge provides much-needed social proof for skilled trade services.
* **Sticky Navigation:** The header keeps the phone number visible at all times, ensuring the user is never more than one click away from a call.

**What Doesn't (The "Friction")**

* **Layout Crowding:** The "Perkhidmatan Kami" (Our Services) cards feel slightly generic with excessive white space that pushes valuable content further down the fold.
* **Stock Photo Contrast:** The "Galeri Projek" (Project Gallery) uses images that look like high-end stock photos; for a local electrician, raw photos of actual local sites (e.g., a DB box in a known shopping mall) usually convert better.
* **Text Heavy FAQ:** The "Soalan Lazim" section at the bottom is presented in a dense multi-column text block which is difficult to scan on mobile devices.

**The Visual DNA**

* **Patterns:** Bento-style service cards, alternating dark/light section backgrounds, and a persistent WhatsApp floating action button.
* **Mood:** **"Reliable-Industrial"** — The yellow and black palette (Hazard/Safety colors) psychologically signals electrical expertise and industrial compliance.

**Implementation Ideas (For My Projects)**

* **The "Keep" List:**
* **Dual CTA Strategy:** Always offer a "Request Quote" form alongside a "WhatsApp Us" button for instant gratification.
* **Experience Badges:** Overlaying "Years of Experience" or "Projects Completed" on real images to build immediate technical authority.


* **The "Fix" List:**
* **Brutalist-Elite Polish:** Replace standard cards with **Shadcn/UI Glassmorphism** effects and use **Cal Sans** for the "Pakar Elektrik" heading to give it a more premium, modern engineering "vibe." 


* **Interactive FAQ:** Transition the dense FAQ text into a **Shadcn/UI Accordion** component to save vertical space and improve scannability.
* **Dynamic Gallery:** Use a simple **Tailwind CSS Grid** with a lightbox feature for the gallery to allow users to zoom in on technical work details.



---

**Strategy Note:** For an SME in this sector, ensure the **PDPA compliance** footer is visible, especially when collecting phone numbers via the "Hubungi Kami" form, to align with 2024 regulatory updates.
