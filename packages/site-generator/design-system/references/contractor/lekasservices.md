## lekasservices.com.jpg

**What Works (The "Wins")**

* **Authenticity Assets:** The inclusion of a real photo of a staff member (top left) and on-site operational photos provides immediate "Human Trust," which is vital for the Malaysian cleaning/facility management sector.
* **Blue-Chip Social Proof:** The "Client Database" section featuring recognizable logos like KIA and MyMydin acts as a high-value trust anchor for B2B procurement officers.
* **Service Breadth:** The 3x2 color-coded grid and gallery sections quickly communicate that the company handles various scales of work, from general cleaning to specialized services.

**What Doesn't (The "Friction")**

* **Typography Nightmare:** The use of script/cursive fonts for headings is a significant accessibility and legibility barrier, especially on mobile. It clashes with the "industrial" nature of the service.
* **Extreme Color Contrast:** The jump between high-saturation yellow and red sections creates "visual fatigue." It feels aggressive rather than professional or approachable.
* **Center-Alignment Fatigue:** The heavy reliance on centered, thin white text over busy backgrounds makes the content nearly impossible to scan quickly.
* **Lack of Direct CTA:** There is no prominent WhatsApp floating button or high-contrast "Request Quote" trigger above the fold, missing out on immediate lead generation.

**The Visual DNA**

* **Patterns:** Alternating full-width color blocks, image-heavy gallery grids, and logo marquees.
* **Mood:** **"Functional-Legacy"** — it looks like an established local business that hasn't updated its digital storefront in a decade.

**Implementation Ideas (For My Projects)**

* **The "Keep" List:**
* **Real Personnel Photos:** Continue using authentic team photos to differentiate from "stock-only" competitors.
* **The Logo Marquee:** A dedicated section for "Trusted By" remains a non-negotiable for B2B-focused SME sites.


* **The "Fix" List:**
* **Brutalist-Elite Realignment:** Swap all script fonts for **Cal Sans** for headings. Transition to a strict left-alignment strategy for all body text to improve readability.
* **Modern Tech Stack:** Rebuild the contact section using **Shadcn/UI** forms and **Tailwind CSS** to create a clean, high-contrast lead capture area that doesn't feel like a 2010s template.
* **Premium Palette:** Replace the harsh red/yellow blocks with a more refined, high-contrast logic (e.g., `zinc-950` backgrounds with single-color accents) to achieve an "Elite" look.
* **Compliance & Localization:** Integrate a clear **English/BM language toggle** and ensure the footer explicitly mentions **PDPA compliance**, especially given the focus on corporate clients.
