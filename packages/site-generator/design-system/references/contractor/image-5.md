**What Works (The "Wins")**

* **Heritage as a Trust Signal:** Explicitly stating "Since 1973" in the hero section provides immediate longevity-based trust, which is highly effective for Malaysian SME services where "fly-by-night" contractors are a concern.
* **High-Contrast CTAs:** The "Start Here" buttons in vibrant red against dark backgrounds ensure the primary conversion path is never lost.
* **Action-Oriented Navigation:** The inclusion of a secondary "Start Here" button in the header utility bar captures intent regardless of scroll position.

**What Doesn't (The "Friction")**

* **Poor Readability Overlays:** The yellow-bordered text boxes sitting over busy images create significant visual noise; the thin white text on these boxes is a strain to read.
* **Dated Desktop Logic:** The layout relies on heavy, dark background textures and fixed-width containers that feel like a mid-2010s template rather than a modern, "Premium" edge-native site.
* **Incomplete Content:** The placeholder text in the "Unique Designs" section ("Replace this content with your own...") is a critical "Trust Killer" that suggests a lack of attention to detail.

**The Visual DNA**

* **Patterns:** Offset image/text blocks, thin gold-bordered feature boxes, and a triple-column footer gallery.
* **Mood:** **"Old-School Industrial"** — it feels established and rugged, but lacks the "Brutalist-Elite" sophistication of modern technical architecture.

**Implementation Ideas (For My Projects)**

* **The "Keep" List:**
* **The "Since [Year]" Hook:** Always integrate the business's founding year or a "Total Projects Completed" stat in the hero to anchor trust.
* **Red/Black Contrast:** This high-visibility logic works well for industrial or technical services; implement using Tailwind `bg-red-600` and `bg-zinc-950`.


* **The "Fix" List:**
* **Brutalist-Elite Realignment:** Swap the serif and thin sans-serif fonts for **Cal Sans** headings. Move to a strict left-alignment with no centered text and replace the gold borders with sharp, high-contrast black/white division lines.
* **Localization & Compliance:** Ensure the presence of a **BM/English toggle** and a persistent WhatsApp CTA, as RM-based SME clients expect immediate chat accessibility.
* **Privacy by Design:** Add a visible **PDPA consent** footer, especially for lead-gen forms, to align with the Malaysian 2024 PDPA amendments and July 2026 e-invoicing standards.
