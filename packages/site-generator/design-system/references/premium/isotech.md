## Isotech.jpg

**What Works (The "Wins")**

* **Niche Authority Headline:** The hero section immediately anchors the brand with "Your Air Cond Expert," providing instant clarity for high-intent search traffic.
* **Service Visualization:** The "Our Services" grid uses real-world imagery (installation, maintenance) which builds functional trust over generic vector illustrations.
* **Integrated Lead Capture:** Having the "Schedule An Appointment Now" form directly above the footer reduces the steps a user needs to take to convert.
* **Local Map Presence:** Including a Google Maps embed in the contact section signals a physical, local presence in Malaysia, which is a major trust factor.

**What Doesn't (The "Friction")**

* **Legibility Crisis:** The yellow text on light/white backgrounds in the "Core Values" and "Expert" sections has very low contrast, making it nearly impossible for users (especially those in your late-50s demographic) to read comfortably.
* **Disconnected Hero CTA:** There is no prominent, high-contrast button (like a green WhatsApp trigger) in the immediate hero viewport, forcing users to scroll to find a way to contact.
* **Layout Gaps:** The "Core Values" section is very text-heavy and lacks a clear visual hierarchy, leading to "wall of text" fatigue.

**The Visual DNA**

* **Patterns:** Large-scale typography, 3-column icon grids, and asymmetric image/text pairings.
* **Mood:** **"Approachable-Modern"** — it tries to feel cleaner than a traditional contractor site but sacrifices usability for aesthetic "vibe."

**Implementation Ideas (For My Projects)**

* **The "Keep" List:**
* **Asymmetric Product Showcases:** Steal the layout of the "Top Notch Air Cond" section for high-end project highlights using Tailwind's `flex-col lg:flex-row`.
* **Map-Form Duo:** Combining the physical map with a contact form in a single high-contrast section for a "one-stop" conversion zone.


* **The "Fix" List:**
* **Brutalist-Elite Realignment:** Swap the yellow headers for **Cal Sans** in solid black or dark zinc. Use a strict high-contrast color logic (no more yellow on white).
* **Vibe-Coding Optimization:** Replace the core values list with a **Shadcn/UI Accordion** or high-contrast cards (`bg-zinc-950` with white text) to make them scannable.
* **Local Trust Injection:** Add a persistent **BM/English language toggle** in the header. Ensure the footer explicitly mentions **PDPA compliance** and preparation for the **July 2026 e-invoicing mandate** to appeal to corporate SME clients.
* **Functional CTA:** Add a floating WhatsApp button with a pre-filled message: "Hi Isotech, I need an aircond service quote."
