# Content Strategy — Pintarweb (v3)

**Campaign:** Nampak — Client Acquisition for Aircond Contractors
**Framing rule:** Every piece of content answers "how does this help contractors get more customers?" If it doesn't lead with customer acquisition, don't publish it.

---

## 1. Content Pillars

### Pillar 1: The Hidden Customers (40% of content)

**Why:** Contractors don't know they're leaving money on the table. Every month, hundreds of people in their area search for aircond service — and none find them.

**Keywords:** "cara dapat pelanggan aircond", "aircond service cheras dapat customer", "bisnes aircond slow", "macam mana nak dapat customer baru"

**Topic cluster:**

```
The Hidden Customers (hub)
├── "290 orang search 'aircond service cheras' sebulan. Contractor sini dapat 0." ← leads with lost customers
├── "Berapa customer awak hilang sebulan tanpa sedar?" ← leads with money
├── "Pesaing awak dapat customer baru sebab diorang nampak dulu" ← leads with competition
├── "Satu job chemical wash RM 180-350. RM 149/bulan — satu job dah cover." ← leads with math
├── "Customer baru Google dulu sebelum call sesiapa" ← leads with behavior
└── "Post FB abang dulu 50 like, sekarang 5 — Facebook sorok. Website = abang control." ← Kena Tipu angle
```

**Rule:** Never lead with "website." Lead with "customer" or "duit."

---

### Pillar 2: Contractor Business Reality (25% of content)

**Why:** Shows we understand the contractor world. Speaks their language about what actually matters — slow days, customer habits, competition.

**Buyer stage:** Awareness + Consideration

**Topic cluster:**

```
Contractor Business Reality (hub)
├── "Macam mana contractor aircond dapat customer in 2026?" ← leads with customer source
├── "Instagram 2,000 followers tapi bisnes still slow — kenapa?" ← leads with business problem
├── "Word-of-mouth tak cukup dah. Customer baru tak datang bergolek." ← leads with growth
├── "5 tahun contractor, masih tunggu customer call. Ada jalan keluar?" ← leads with pain
└── "Facebook bukan kedai abang — website kedai sendiri. Bila algorithm tukar, abang tak hilang apa-apa." ← Platform ownership angle
```

---

### Pillar 3: How We Get You Customers (20% of content)

**Why:** Demystifies the mechanism. Shows contractors how a Pintarweb subscriber actually gets found.

**Buyer stage:** Consideration

**Topic cluster:**

```
How We Get You Customers (hub)
├── "Dari tak nampak terus dapat customer — macam mana ni jadi?" ← leads with transformation
├── "5 hari — from audit to live. Lepas tu Google mula tunjuk." ← leads with speed
├── "Apa jadi bila customer search bisnes awak? Kita tunjuk." ← leads with customer perspective
└── "Website bukan benda fancy. Ianya macam papan tanda kedai — tapi online." ← leads with familiar analogy
```

---

### Pillar 4: Real Contractors, Real Results (15% of content)

**Why:** Social proof. Other contractors like them are getting customers.

**Buyer stage:** Decision

**Topic cluster:**

```
Real Contractors, Real Results (hub)
├── "Contractor A dapat 3 new customer bulan pertama lepas join Pintarweb" ← leads with customer count
├── "Sebelum: bisnes slow. Selepas: Google bagi dia 5 leads seminggu." ← leads with before/after
└── "3 bulan dengan Pintarweb — berapa customer extra? Owner cerita sendiri." ← leads with real story
```
