# Referral Playbook — Phase 3: TIANG (v4)

**Weeks 9-12 | Theme:** Convert. Prove. Activate referrals.
**v4 pricing:** RM 149/month. Referral reward: 1 month free (RM 149).
**Aligned with:** Business plan referral programme.

---

## 1. Supplier Partnership Model

### The Deal
You refer a contractor who wants more customers → you get **1 month free (RM 149)**.
Contractor pays RM 149/month (3-month advance RM 447). You get 1 month free.

### The Pitch (In-Person Visit)

Walk in, buy something small (RM 10-20 part). Don't pitch immediately.

```
You: "Bos, saya Yus. Saya bantu contractor aircond around sini
dapat lebih customer. Contractor [name] baru-baru ni dah start
nampak hasil — dulu bisnes slow, sekarang call masuk.
Nak tengok kejap?"

[Show on phone — 30 seconds: Razif's site + Google presence]

"Benda ni contractor bayar RM 149 sebulan — 3 bulan advance.
Tapi satu job chemical wash (RM 180-350) dah cover. Auto-reply
WhatsApp tangkap lead masa diorang kerja.

Saya boleh bagi 1 bulan free (RM 149) untuk setiap contractor
yang awak refer dan signup.

Kalau ada customer contractor datang cakap bisnes slow — just
pass my number. Saya urus."

[Leave simple card with: name + phone + "Nak lebih customer? WhatsApp saya"]
```

### The Follow-Up System

| Time | Action | Tool |
|------|--------|------|
| Day 1 | Visit + leave card | In-person |
| Day 3 | WhatsApp: "Bos, ada contractor cakap pasal customer kurang?" | WhatsApp |
| Week 2 | Call/message: "Contractor [name] dah mula dapat customer baru" | WhatsApp |
| Month 1 | Visit again: buy another small part, mention a success story | In-person |
| Month 2 | If zero referrals — deprioritize, focus on new shops | — |

## 2. Client Referral Ask

### When a subscriber signs up

```
"Eh, before tu — you ada kawan contractor yang selalu cakap
bisnes slow? Maybe diorang pun boleh guna sistem ni.
Nak saya call diorang, atau awak nak bagitahu dulu?
    Dapat 1 bulan free (RM 149) untuk awak."
```

### When a prospect says NO

```
"Faham. Since awak dalam field ni — awak mesti kenal contractor
lain yang selalu complain customer kurang. Kalau diorang berminat,
boleh refer — saya bagi 1 bulan free (RM 149).
Awak tolong diorang, saya bagi duit."
```

### When a prospect doesn't reply after 2 follow-ups

```
"Nampaknya awak maybe belum ready. No worries. Tapi kalau
awak ada kawan contractor yang nak lebih customer — hantar
my number. Saya uruskan. Dapat 1 bulan free (RM 149)."
```

### At every supplier visit

```
"Contractor mana paling rajin datang sini — yang selalu
cakap pasal customer kurang?"
```
