# Facebook Group Strategy — Phase 1 (v2)

**Goal:** Build presence in 3-5 groups where aircond contractors actually hang out.
**Time:** 10 min/day (comment) + 15 min/week (one original post)
**Tone:** Conversational BM. Like talking to a fellow contractor. No formal Malay.
**Status:** Primary platform (60% of content time)

## 2. Daily Routine (10 min)

### Step 1: Scroll feed (3 min)

Look for:
- Complaints about slow business / fewer calls
- Questions about getting more customers
- Discussions about Google, online presence, websites
- Contractors sharing their IG or FB page asking for follows

### Step 2: Comment on 2 posts (5 min)

**Rules:**
- Add value. Don't just "nice" or agree.
- If someone asks for advice: give genuine answer first
- Relate it to your observation if relevant
- No links. No self-promotion.
- Reference your research naturally — "Saya perhatikan kat area Cheras..." not "Saya ada buat research..."

**v3 rule:** Every comment must lead with **customer acquisition** or business pain. Mention website only as the mechanism, never the headline.

**Example comments:**
```
"Betul tu bos. Saya perhatikan contractor area Cheras pun sama masalah —
ramai customer call diorang tapi yang baru Google dulu, diorang tak nampak."
```

```
"Aku lately check competitor kat area Cheras. Diorang dah mula dapat
customer baru dari Google. Yang still tunggu referral je makin senyap."
```

```
"Pernah check tak berapa customer actually search bisnes korang sebulan?
Kat area Cheras 290 orang search 'aircond service cheras' — contractor
sana dapat 0 dari tu. Setiap bulan."
```

```
"Betul tu bos. Aku perasan lately Facebook sorok post bisnes kecik.
Dulu contractor area sini post dapat 50 like — sekarang 5-10 je.
Bukan content teruk. Facebook yang control. Website sendiri — takde
siapa boleh sorok."
```

### Step 3: Post weekly insight (15 min, Wednesday)

Post in **one group per week, rotated.** Never the same post across multiple groups.
Adapt to each group's culture.

**v3 rule:** Lead with **customer acquisition** in every insight. Website is never the headline — it's the mechanism.

**Week 1 insight:**
```
Baru check contractor aircond area Cheras. 290 orang search
'aircond service cheras' sebulan. Contractor sini dapat 0 dari tu.
Setiap bulan ada orang nak pasang aircond, repair, servis —
tapi diorang tak jumpa contractor sini. Fikir-fikirkan.
```

**Week 2 insight:**
```
Pernah tak korang Google bisnes sendiri? Cuba buat sekarang.
Apa yang nampak? Kalau tak nampak — maksudnya setiap hari ada
customer potential cari korang tapi tak jumpa. Rugi tu.
```

**Week 3 insight:**
```
Ramai contractor still fikir cukup dengan IG. Tapi customer baru
search Google dulu — bukan IG. IG nampak kat followers je.
Google nampak kat semua orang yang search. Dua benda ni lain.
Korang dah Google-optimized ke?
```

**Week 4 insight:**
```
Sebulan lepas I mula perhati contractor area Cheras/Kajang.
95% tahu diorang kena buat something supaya customer baru dapat
cari diorang. Tapi takut — mahal, susah, tak tau mula mana.

Padahal competitor yang dapat customer baru sekarang bukan
sebab hebat — tapi sebab diorang mula dulu.

Satu lagi: ramai bergantung kat FB/IG untuk customer.
Tapi platform tu bukan kedai korang. Bila algorithm tukar —
reach drop, inquiry hilang. Website = kedai sendiri.

Yang penting: satu job chemical wash RM 180-350 dah cukup
cover sebulan. Auto-reply WhatsApp tangkap lead masa korang
atas bumbung. Fikir pasal tu.
```

## 3. Golden Rules

| Rule | Why |
|------|-----|
| **Comment on 2 posts before posting your own** | Groups ban self-promotion fast. Earn presence first. |
| **No "RM 149/month" in comments** | Only DM if someone asks directly. |
| **No links in first post** | Wait until someone asks or you've built rapport. |
| **Never DM from group** | Engage publicly. Let them come to you. |
| **BM only** (mix with English is fine) | Contractors speak BM among themselves. English sounds corporate. |
| **1 emoji max per message** | More looks like spam. |
| **Rotate groups weekly** | Don't spam the same group. Spread presence. |

---

## 4. What NOT to Do

- ❌ Don't paste a LinkedIn post into FB. Different tone. Different audience.
- ❌ Don't post a link cold. Groups block links. Wait until someone asks.
- ❌ Don't argue with comments. If someone disagrees: "Faham bro, pendapat lain. Ok je."
- ❌ Don't be the "awkward promoter." You're a fellow contractor sharing observations.
- ❌ Don't post the same thing in multiple groups. Write original per group.
- ❌ Don't pitch in comments. Ever. DM only.
