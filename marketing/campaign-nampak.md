## 1. Strategic Frame (REVISED)

**Category claim:** Business growth partner for aircond contractors. We help you get more customers — a website is how we deliver it.

**ICP:** Aircond contractor / owner-operator, Cheras-Kajang-Ampang-Bangi, 30-50yo, Malay/male, 1-5 staff. Gets leads from repeat customers and WhatsApp referrals. Says "bisnes slow" when business is quiet. Doesn't know where his next customer is coming from. RM 150-300 average job value.

**Core problem:** Contractors rely on word-of-mouth and repeat customers. But new customers search Google first. Every month, 290+ people in Cheras search for aircond service — and most contractors never get found. The result: bisnes slow, customer sikit, pendapatan tak menentu. This isn't a website problem. It's a **lead generation problem**.

**Brand voice:** Conversational BM with English mix. Never formal Malay. "Boleh" not "bolehkah". "Nak" not "ingin". Like explaining to a fellow contractor over WhatsApp. Focus on what matters to them: **customer, duit, bisnes.** Not websites, not tech.

### Sales Architecture: Anchor & Pivot

The outreach follows a deliberate anchoring strategy:

1. **Quote RM 800 one-time** for the standalone website (genuine, buildable offer)
2. **Pivot to subscription:** "Atau RM 149/bulan — website FREE, auto-reply WhatsApp, Google Business semua included. 3 bulan advance (RM 447). Satu job chemical wash (RM 180-350) dah cover. Mana lagi masuk akal?"
3. **The RM 800 anchor makes RM 149 look smart**, not expensive. This is not a bait-and-switch — the RM 800 option is real.

### How to Frame

| Context | Framing |
|---------|---------|
| Cold outreach Touch 3+ | "RM 800 one-time untuk website. Atau RM 149/bulan — website free, auto-reply bot tangkap lead masa awak atas bumbung. Satu chemical wash dah cover." |
| Interest/"Berapa harga?" | "Satu job chemical wash RM 180-350. Sebulan RM 149 — auto-reply WhatsApp je dah boleh cover balik modal." |
| Against competitor pricing | "Freelance web designer charge RM 500-2000, lepas handover takde support. Kita RM 149/bulan termasuk everything — SEO, GMB, bot, hosting." |

**Rule:** Never lead with price. Lead with **"berapa customer awak hilang sebulan?"** Then price feels small.
