# Outreach Message Templates — PintarWeb

WhatsApp templates for prospect outreach. BM = Bahasa Melayu, EN = English.

---

## Variables

Replace these in every message:
| Variable | Example |
|----------|---------|
| `{name}` | Ah Seng |
| `{business_name}` | Ah Seng Plumbing |
| `{demo_url}` | https://preview.pintarweb.com/ah-seng-plumbing/?ref=outreach&prospect=xyz |

## Tips

1. **Personalize** — Use their name and business name
2. **Don't be pushy** — Friendly, helpful tone
3. **Send at good times** — 9-11am or 7-9pm local time
4. **Include audit URL** — Adds credibility (if available)
5. **Track in spreadsheet** — Name, business, sent date, response, status
